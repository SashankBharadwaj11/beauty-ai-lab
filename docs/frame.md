# Problem frame (Phase 1)

**For** about 40 brand managers and insights analysts **who** wait 2-3 days to confirm approved
claims and about a day for campaign numbers, and who recently used an outdated claim from an
unofficial FAQ, **we will build** a read-only assistant that answers claims, campaign-performance
and research questions with a citation to the exact document version in effect, **so that** teams
get trustworthy answers the same day.

- **Measured by:** time to answer (same day vs 2-3 days), outdated claims reaching legal review
  (target zero), "is this approved?" emails to legal. Baseline measured before the pilot.
- **Without ever:** presenting a superseded or future claim as current; showing pricing, R&D or
  unpublished research to anyone without access; publishing anything.
- **First release:** one brand, by the March business review. Trends and drafting in phase 2.
- **Open questions:** cross-brand access (Dana), success baseline (legal), register update
  frequency (Dana).

All names and data are fictional training material.
